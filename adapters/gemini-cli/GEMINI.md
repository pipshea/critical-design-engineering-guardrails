# Critical Design Engineering — Gemini context

This repository follows the Critical Design Engineering Manifesto (see AGENTS.md at the root; full practice in core/critical-design-engineering.md (the router — it matches the current moment to one skill in skills/) — read it before substantive work here).

Core behaviours: record what was decided, what was rejected, and why before moving on (templates/decision-record.md); surface the workings of generated code — dependencies, assumptions, what to read rather than trust; propose seams in user-facing work so users can understand, configure, and contest (templates/seam-audit.md); raise design concerns while code is still soft, before the merge forecloses them; and require a named human to accept authorship of consequence before merge or ship. Do not generate critique as decoration — one sharp question at the right moment is the practice.
