# Provotype Brief

<!-- Statements 8 & 10: carry critique into the repository, where it must survive contact with use. -->
<!-- For artifacts whose job is to hold a question open — provocations, desirability prototypes, deliberate seams. -->
<!-- Write this when the artifact is made; revisit it at every merge that touches it. -->

## The artifact

What is it, and where does it live in the product or codebase?

## The question it holds open

State it as a question a person could disagree about. "Should this exist?" is a valid answer-shape; "does it work?" is not — that is a different document.

## Who the question is for

Which users, stakeholders, or team members should feel this question when they encounter the artifact?

## How we will know it is still working

What does it look like when this artifact is provoking as intended — what conversations, objections, or configuration choices should we see?

## How we will know delivery has domesticated it

The specific signs that the question has been filed into "just another feature": the provocation renamed to something neutral, the seam removed for polish, the default hardened, the objections stopped. Name them now, while you can still recognise them.

## The merge check (revisit at every merge that touches this artifact)

- Does the question still exist in the shipped artifact, or only in this document?
- What did the latest merge close?
- **Decision:** keep holding the question open / deliberately close it (record why in a Decision Record) / the artifact has been domesticated — acknowledge it rather than pretending otherwise.
