**Date:** YYYY-MM-DD

### What we decided

One or two sentences. The what, stated plainly.

### What we rejected

The live alternatives, each in a line. If nothing was rejected, say so — a decision with no alternatives considered is worth noticing.

### Why

The reasoning. This is the section the code cannot hold and the agent cannot recover. Include:
- The constraint, evidence, or belief that tipped the decision
- Any assumption that, if it turned out false, should reopen this decision

### Who is affected

Beyond the people in the room: which users, teams, or downstream systems live with this choice.

### What this closes, and what stays open

What questions does this decision settle? What did we deliberately leave open, and where does that open question now live?
